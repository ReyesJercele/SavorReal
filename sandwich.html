Sandwich

Golden, buttery toasted bread layered with melted cheese, savory cured meats, and fresh basil leaves.

Ingredients
  • 2 slices sandwich bread or sourdough
  • 2 to 3 slices mozzarella or any regular cheese
  • 3 to 4 slices salami or pepperoni (Option)
  • 2 to 3 slices ham or bacon (Option)
  • 3 to 4 fresh basil leaves or any
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons softened butter or mayonnaise
Cooking Instructions

1. Spread butter or mayonnaise on the outer sides of the bread slices. 2. Layer cheese, meats, and basil between the slices. 3. Toast in a skillet over medium-low heat until bread is golden brown and cheese is fully melted.
