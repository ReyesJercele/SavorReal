Brownies

Rich, fudgy, and chewy chocolate brownies cooked to perfection without needing an oven.

Ingredients
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder (e.g., Ricoa or unsweetened cocoa powder)
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar (or white sugar)
  • 1 piece medium egg
  • 1/2 cup melted margarine (e.g., Star Margarine) or cooking oil
  • 1/4 cup condensed milk or Bear Brand evaporated milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional)
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
Cooking Instructions

1. Whisk melted margarine, sugar, egg, and condensed milk together. 2. Fold in flour, cocoa powder, salt, and vanilla until smooth. 3. Pour batter into a greased pan and steam or bake on low heat until set and fudgy.
