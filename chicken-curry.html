Chicken Curry

A comforting Filipino classic style featuring juicy chicken and colorful vegetables bathed in a rich, aromatic coconut curry broth.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 kg chicken (mixed parts)
  • 2 medium potatoes (cubed)
  • 1 medium carrot (sliced)
  • 1 small onion (chopped)
  • 3 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 thumb-sized ginger (sliced thin)
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons curry powder
  • 1 pack coconut milk powder or 1 small can coconut milk
  • 1.5 cups water
  • 1 tablespoon fish sauce or salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
Cooking Instructions

1. Sauté garlic, onion, and ginger in oil until aromatic. 2. Add chicken pieces and cook until color turns white. Stir in curry powder. 3. Add potatoes, carrots, and water. Cover and simmer until tender. 4. Pour in coconut milk and fish sauce. Simmer gently for 5 minutes until sauce thickens.
