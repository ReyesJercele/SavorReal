Garlic Pasta

Rich pasta with garlic, parmesan, and a smooth sauce.

Ingredients
  • 200g pasta
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 cup cream
  • Salt and black pepper
Cooking Instructions

1. Boil pasta in salted water until al dente. 2. Sauté minced garlic in cooking oil over medium heat until fragrant. 3. Pour in cream and simmer gently until thickened. 4. Toss cooked pasta into the garlic cream sauce, season with salt and pepper, and serve hot.
