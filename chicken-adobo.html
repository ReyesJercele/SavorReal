Chicken Adobo

Classic Filipino chicken adobo braised in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and bay leaves until savory, tangy, and tender.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 kilo chicken (neck, wings, or mixed cuts) or pork kasim/liempo
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce (like Silver Swan or Datu Puti)
  • 3 tablespoons cane vinegar or coconut vinegar
  • 1 head garlic (crushed and chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  • 2 pieces dried bay leaves (dahon ng laurel)
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional, to balance taste)
  • 1 small onion (optional, to stretch the meal)
Cooking Instructions

1. Combine chicken, soy sauce, garlic, peppercorns, bay leaves, and water in a pot. Marinate for 15-30 minutes. 2. Bring to a boil, then simmer over medium-low heat for 20 minutes until chicken is tender. 3. Pour in vinegar and do not stir. Simmer for another 10 minutes. 4. Heat oil in a pan, sear the cooked chicken pieces until golden, then pour the sauce back to reduce. Serve with warm rice.
